Featured Axolotl Fact

Axolotls are sometimes called "Mexican walking fish," but they're actually salamanders that never leave the water! They keep their juvenile features throughout their entire lives, including those adorable feathery gills.

Quick Care Tip

Water Temperature: Keep your axolotl's tank between 60-64°F (15-18°C). They prefer cooler water than most aquarium animals!
